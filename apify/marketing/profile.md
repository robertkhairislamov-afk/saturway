I build Apify Actors that turn public websites into clean, structured data: numbers as numbers, one row per record, the same fields across sources. You pay only for results, with no start fees.

**Job scrapers**, one output format for all: title, company, locations, remote, salary as numbers, description, apply link and dates.

- [All-in-One Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/all-in-one-jobs-scraper): LinkedIn and 12 more job sites in one search, without duplicates
- [LinkedIn Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/linkedin-jobs-scraper): public job listings without login, with full job details
- [Reed Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/reed-jobs-scraper): the UK
- [InfoJobs Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/infojobs-jobs-scraper): Spain
- [StepStone Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/stepstone-jobs-scraper): Germany
- [Welcome to the Jungle Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/welcome-to-the-jungle-jobs-scraper): France, Europe, the UK and the US
- [SEEK Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/seek-jobs-scraper): Australia and New Zealand
- [Jobstreet Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/jobstreet-jobs-scraper): Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines and Indonesia
- [JobsDB Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/jobsdb-jobs-scraper): Hong Kong and Thailand
- [Dice Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/dice-jobs-scraper): US tech jobs
- [Greenhouse](https://apify.com/robertosan16/greenhouse-jobs-scraper), [Lever](https://apify.com/robertosan16/lever-jobs-scraper), [Ashby](https://apify.com/robertosan16/ashby-jobs-scraper) and [Workday](https://apify.com/robertosan16/workday-jobs-scraper) Jobs Scrapers: every job of any company's career site, on Workday even past the 2,000 search limit

- [Redfin Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/redfin-scraper): US homes for sale, sold homes and rentals, with all Redfin filters

**Apps and news**

- [Google Play Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/google-play-scraper): top charts, search rankings and app details
- [Google News Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/google-news-scraper): news by keyword, topic and country

**Why these Actors**

- Pay only for results, no start fees
- Only new records since the last run, for alerts
- Works with Make, Zapier, n8n and AI agents (MCP)
- Found a bug or need a field? Open an issue on the Actor page, I reply quickly
