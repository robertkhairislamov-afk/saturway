### Clean data from job sites, real estate and app stores

I build Apify Actors that turn public websites into clean, structured data: one row per record, numbers as numbers, the same fields across sources. Pay only for results, no start fees.

**Jobs**, one output format for all sites:

- [All-in-One Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/all-in-one-jobs-scraper): LinkedIn, Indeed and 12 more sites in one search
- [LinkedIn](https://apify.com/robertosan16/linkedin-jobs-scraper), [Indeed](https://apify.com/robertosan16/indeed-jobs-scraper) (62 countries), [Reed](https://apify.com/robertosan16/reed-jobs-scraper) (UK), [InfoJobs](https://apify.com/robertosan16/infojobs-jobs-scraper) (Spain), [StepStone](https://apify.com/robertosan16/stepstone-jobs-scraper) (Germany), [Welcome to the Jungle](https://apify.com/robertosan16/welcome-to-the-jungle-jobs-scraper) (Europe), [Dice](https://apify.com/robertosan16/dice-jobs-scraper) (US tech)
- [SEEK](https://apify.com/robertosan16/seek-jobs-scraper) (Australia, NZ), [Jobstreet](https://apify.com/robertosan16/jobstreet-jobs-scraper) (Southeast Asia), [JobsDB](https://apify.com/robertosan16/jobsdb-jobs-scraper) (Hong Kong, Thailand)
- Career sites: [Greenhouse](https://apify.com/robertosan16/greenhouse-jobs-scraper), [Lever](https://apify.com/robertosan16/lever-jobs-scraper), [Ashby](https://apify.com/robertosan16/ashby-jobs-scraper), [Workday](https://apify.com/robertosan16/workday-jobs-scraper)

**Real estate**: [Redfin Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/redfin-scraper), US homes for sale, sold and for rent

**Apps and news**: [Google Play Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/google-play-scraper), [Google News Scraper](https://apify.com/robertosan16/google-news-scraper)

**Why these Actors**

- Only new records since the last run, for daily alerts
- Works with Make, Zapier, n8n and AI agents (MCP)
- Need a field? Open an issue on the Actor page, I reply quickly
